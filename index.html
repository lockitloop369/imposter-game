How to play Imposter

Imposter is a free word party game for 3 or more players. Everyone gets the same secret word, except the imposters, who have to bluff their way through.

  1. Type your name. There is no login and no account. A name is all you need.
  2. Get your word. Each player privately sees the secret word. Imposters only learn that they are the imposter, plus a hint if the host allows one.
  3. Give clues. Taking turns, everyone says one clue word. Each player gives at least two clues, with 10 seconds per word.
  4. Discuss and vote. Talk it over, then everyone votes on their own phone for who they think the imposter is.
  5. Last chance. If every imposter is caught, one of them gets a single guess at the secret word to steal the win.
  6. The referee decides. The game announces who won, shows the word each player got, and checks every clue against the secret word. The host can correct a check.

Ways to play

  • Online with friends: create a private room and share the 5-letter code or the invite link.
  • Quick play: join a public room with other players.
  • In person: pass one phone around the group.
  • With AI players: short of people? Add AI players in any mode. They say their clues out loud, vote and guess like everyone else, and they are never told who the imposter is.
  • As an app: add Imposter to your phone's home screen and it opens full screen, like any other app.

Online rooms have voice chat, video chat and text chat, and bigger groups get more imposters. Secret words come in English, Chinese (中文), Spanish (Español), Hindi (हिन्दी), Bengali (বাংলা), Telugu (తెలుగు), Tamil (தமிழ்), Marathi (मराठी), Arabic (العربية), French (Français), Portuguese (Português) and Russian (Русский). It runs in the browser, with no download and no sign-up.
